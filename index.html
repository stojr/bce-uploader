BCE Comics Pod Uploader

A private, single-user tool that publishes our own podcast episodes and clips to our own YouTube channel.

What it does

BCE Comics Pod is a comics review podcast. Each recorded episode is cut into per-issue clips and short-form versions, and each one needs a title, a description, tags, a caption track and a thumbnail. This tool takes the files and copy we have already produced and uploads them to our own channel, scheduling each one for its release time.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not distributed, not sold, and has no sign-up. It runs on one computer and signs in to one Google account — the one that owns the channel.

What it accesses

  • youtube.upload — uploading our own videos and setting their thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl — attaching our own caption tracks and adding our videos to our own playlists.
  • youtube.readonly — reading our own upload list and public view counts for our internal tracking page.

It never touches any channel or account other than our own, and it collects nothing about viewers. The privacy policy sets this out in full.
