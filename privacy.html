Privacy Policy — BCE Comics Pod Uploader

Last updated 31 October 2026

What this application is

BCE Comics Pod Uploader is a private, single-user tool used by the owners of the BCE Comics Pod YouTube channel to publish our own episodes and clips to that same channel. It is not distributed, not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and has no users other than the channel owner.

The same workflow includes BCE Poster, our Meta app, which publishes our own short videos and announcement posts to our own Facebook Page and our own Instagram account. It is private and single-user in the same way; it is covered below. Everything on this page applies to both unless a section names one.

Who we are

BCE Comics Pod. Contact: bcecomicspod@gmail.com

What the application accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the application requests these scopes, and uses them only against that signed-in owner's own YouTube channel:

  • youtube.upload — to upload our own episode and clip files and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to attach the caption tracks we generate for our own videos and to add those videos to our own playlists.
  • youtube.readonly — to read our own upload list and our own public view counts, so our internal tracking page knows which episodes are already published.

What is stored, and where

  • An OAuth refresh token, stored as a file on the channel owner's own computer. It is not stored on any server, and there is no server.
  • Video titles, descriptions, tags, caption files and thumbnail images that we wrote and produced ourselves, held locally as part of our ordinary production workflow.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, process or infer any information about viewers of our channel, or about any Google account other than the channel owner's own. The application contains no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no advertising, no database and no server component.

Sharing and disclosure

No data is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party. The YouTube uploader contacts only Google's own API endpoints. BCE Poster contacts only Meta's own API endpoints, plus the private storage described below, which holds nothing but our own video files.

Limited Use

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy describes how Google handles data.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke this application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also stops the application immediately.

Facebook and Instagram: BCE Poster

BCE Poster is used only by the owner of the BCE Comics Pod Facebook Page, signed in with Facebook Login for Business. It acts only on that Page and on the Instagram professional account linked to it, and uses the access it is granted to:

  • publish our own short videos (Reels) and our own photos and posts to that Page and that Instagram account, either at once or scheduled for a later time;
  • read back the processing and publishing status of what we posted, so a failed upload can be noticed and is never posted twice;
  • read our own Page's and account's basic details, and the play counts of our own videos.

It does not read, store or reply to comments or messages, and it does not access any other Page, account or person's data.

To publish a Reel, Instagram fetches the video file itself. The file is our own finished video, placed for a few minutes in private cloud storage that we control, behind a link that expires after one hour, and removed as soon as Instagram has taken it.

BCE Poster stores a Page access token as a file on the owner's own computer, and a local record of what it has posted (the ids Meta returned and when). Neither is stored on a server. Its use of Meta's APIs follows the Meta Platform Terms; Meta's Privacy Policy describes how Meta handles data.

Retention and deletion

The personal data these tools hold is the local Google OAuth token and the local Meta access token described above, together with the local records of what we published. Each is deleted by deleting its file; no copy exists anywhere else.

To remove BCE Poster's access and data: in Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select BCE Poster and choose Remove; then delete the local token file. Nothing BCE Poster held remains after that. Any other request relating to data can be sent to bcecomicspod@gmail.com, and will be answered within 30 days.

Children

The application is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than the channel owner.
