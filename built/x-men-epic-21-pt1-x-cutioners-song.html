X-Men Epic 21 Pt1: The X-Cutioner's Song

Nick and stojr team-up to read and review two books at once: X-Men Epic Collection vol. 21 and X-Force Epic Collection vol. 2, which both collect The X-Cutioner's Song. Part 1 is everything before the crossover - Uncanny X-Men 289-293 and X-Men 10-13 from the X-Men volume, then Cable: Blood and Metal 1-2 from the X-Force volume. Part 2 covers the crossover itself and the rest of the X-Force book.
